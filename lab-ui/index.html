    Before you start

    Every mission below is a support case, not a query-copying exercise. Fixing the monitor is only half the job. This work looks a lot like what we actually do for customers day to day: understand what they are trying to measure, decide whether they are using the right tool for it, and help them see that for themselves.

    Always start at the source. Before you change anything, open the monitor's query and read exactly what it is measuring, not what you assume it measures.

    Once you think you know what is wrong, prove it instead of just asserting it. A Notebook is a good way to do that: pin the graphs, the query, and a short note about what they show, so the evidence still makes sense after the alert clears. If you haven't used Notebooks before, this is a good place to start: Datadog docs on Notebooks. Keep whatever you build, a Notebook, a saved graph, or a query you can paste back, because we will go over it together afterward.

    Think critically about the customer's setup as you go. A monitor that is technically correct can still be teaching them the wrong lesson about their own service. Part of the job is helping someone use Datadog in a way that actually improves how they work, not just clearing the page in front of you.

    Destroy everything?

    • delete the learning-week-k8s cluster
    • delete every monitor tagged learning-week from your Datadog account
